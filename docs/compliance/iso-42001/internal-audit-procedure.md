# ISO/IEC 42001 — Internal Audit Procedure

## 1. Audit Schedule & Scope
- **Quarterly Reviews:** Re-audit all governance gates, test-integrity checkers, and language pack specs.
- **Continuous Validation:** Automated execution of typechecking, boundary checks, and test suites in CI on every push and pull request.
- **Audit Findings Log:** Any regression or security finding must be recorded in the risk register and addressed with a failing test case before merge.
