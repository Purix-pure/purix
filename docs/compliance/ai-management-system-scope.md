# ISO/IEC 42001 — AI Management System Scope

## 1. Scope Definition
The Artificial Intelligence Management System (AIMS) encompasses the Purix monorepo codebase (`@purix/core`, `purix` (CLI), `@purix/api`, `@purix/mcp-server`), including its deterministic governance gates, verification sandboxes, licensing entitlement verification, and LLM router pipelines.

## 2. Accountability & Roles
- **Lead Maintainer / Engineering Lead:** Accountable for security gate enforcement, cryptographic audit trail integrity, and CI compliance verification.
