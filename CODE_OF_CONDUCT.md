# Code of Conduct

Purix is a developer tool, and this project is for people who want to make it better. Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work.

## What we expect

- Be courteous to everyone, whatever their background or experience.
- Give and take feedback on code and ideas without making it personal.
- Keep issues, discussions and pull requests on topic and in good faith.
- Respect other people's privacy: do not post someone's personal information, and do not paste credentials, tokens or private code into public issues.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language or behaviour.
- Sexualised language or attention, and unwelcome contact.
- Publishing a security vulnerability before a fix is available (see [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md)).
- Spam, or content posted only to promote something unrelated.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere someone is clearly acting on behalf of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report a problem, use GitHub's private reporting flow described in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) and say that it is a conduct report, or contact a maintainer directly through GitHub. Reports are read by a maintainer and handled privately. There is no guaranteed response time.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break this code. Enforcement is the maintainers' judgement; they will try to be fair and to explain what they did.
